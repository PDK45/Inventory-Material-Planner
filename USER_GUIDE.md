# 🚀 MPPMS Executive Portal — System User Guide

Welcome to the **Material Planning and Procurement Management System (MPPMS)**. This guide provides a complete, step-by-step walkthrough of how to use the portal to manage manufacturing material requirements, purchase requisitions, supplier orders, and warehouse inventory movements.

---

## 📌 1. System Overview

MPPMS is an enterprise supply chain management system built to match industrial standards (**SAP S/4HANA & Oracle Cloud SCM**). It automates the end-to-end material flow:

```
[Target Production Plan] 
       ↓
[Bill of Materials (BOM) Breakdown] 
       ↓
[MRP Engine Net Deficiency Check] 
       ↓
[Auto-Generate Purchase Requisition (PR)] 
       ↓
[PR Approval & Purchase Order (PO) Issuance] 
       ↓
[SAP MIGO 101 Goods Receipt] 
       ↓
[Stock Committed to Warehouse Balance]
```

---

## 🌐 2. How to Launch & Switch Modes

### Accessing the Portal
- Open your browser and navigate to:  
  `http://localhost:8000/mppms_portal.html?v=7`

### Toggling Operating Modes
On the bottom-left of the sidebar menu, locate the **Data Sync** toggle:
1. **Demo Mode (Simulated)**: Runs self-contained in your browser using standard test data. Use this for quick testing, demonstrations, or offline review.
2. **Live Mode (Oracle ORDS REST DB)**: Connects directly to the live **Oracle 23ai Database** backend running at `http://localhost:8080/ords/mppms/api`.

## 🛠️ 3. Step-by-Step Module Guide

### Module 1: Dashboard (War-Room Command Center)
The dashboard provides a real-time high-density overview of your plant's logistics:
- **Top Metric Cards**: Displays open Purchase Requisitions (PRs), open Purchase Orders (POs), active stock shortages, and total monthly spend.
- **Shortages Alert List**: Displays items whose stock balance has dropped below safety stock. Click **Reorder** to jump directly to procurement.
- **Pending Approvals**: Shows incoming PRs waiting for executive sign-off. Click **Approve** or **Reject**.
- **Upcoming Deliveries**: Tracks incoming supplier shipments with due dates and values.

---

### Module 2: Bill of Materials (BOM)
Used by production engineers to see what raw materials are needed to manufacture a product.
1. Click **Bill of Materials** in the sidebar.
2. Select a target product (e.g. `PRD-2001 - Industrial Centrifugal Pump`).
3. Enter the batch quantity (e.g. `10 Units`).
4. Click **Calculate BOM Breakdown**.
5. **Result**: The system breaks down the multi-level product tree, displays required component quantities (Steel H-Beams, Bolts, Motors), calculates standard cost roll-ups, and compares requirements against current warehouse stock on-hand.

---

### Module 3: MRP Planner (Material Requirements Planning)
Calculates exact material deficits across active production plans.
1. Click **MRP Planner** in the sidebar.
2. Select a Master Production Plan (e.g. `PLAN-2026-Q3`).
3. Click **Run MRP Calculation**.
4. **Result**: The engine calculates:
   $$\text{Net Deficit} = \text{Gross Requirement} + \text{Safety Stock} - \text{Stock On-Hand} - \text{Stock On-Order}$$
5. If net shortages are found, click **Generate Purchase Requisitions**. The system automatically creates PRs for the missing materials.

---

### Module 4: Procurement Manager (PRs & POs)
Manages the purchasing cycle from requisition to order placement.
1. Click **Procurement** in the sidebar.
2. **Purchase Requisitions (PR Ledger)**:
   - Review pending PRs generated by MRP or submitted by department heads.
   - Click **Approve** on a PR (status changes from `SUBMITTED` to `APPROVED`).
3. **Raising Purchase Orders (POs)**:
   - Click **Raise PO** next to any approved PR.
   - The system uses Approved Supplier List (ASL) scoring to assign a vendor (e.g., Tata Steel, Hindalco) and issues an official Purchase Order (`PO-2026-xxxx`).

---

### Module 5: Goods Movements (SAP MIGO Standard)
Handles physical inventory transactions in the warehouse.
1. Click **Goods Movements** in the sidebar.
2. Select the **Movement Type**:
   - **Movement 101 (PO Goods Receipt)**: Receiving inbound shipments from a vendor. Increases warehouse balance.
   - **Movement 261 (Goods Issue to Production)**: Issuing raw material to the factory floor. Deducts warehouse balance.
   - **Movement 311 (Bin-to-Bin Transfer)**: Relocates material between warehouse bins without changing total stock.
   - **Movement 551 (Scrappage)**: Scraps damaged goods.
3. Select the Material, Warehouse, Storage Bin, and Quantity.
4. Click **Post Goods Movement**.
5. **Result**: The warehouse balance updates instantly, shortage alerts update, and an unalterable audit document (e.g. `MIGO-2026-0043`) is committed to the transaction log.

---

### Module 6: Receiver Goods Bills (RGB)
Used for formal dock receiving and receiving voucher creation.
1. Click **Goods Receipts** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Create New Goods Bill**.
3. Select an open Purchase Order and Supplier.
4. Add line items received at the loading dock.
5. Click **Post & Commit Goods Bill**. This commits the stock directly into warehouse bins.

---

### Module 7: AI Forecasting & Advanced Tools
- **AI Forecasting**: Generates demand predictions and seasonal trend curves.
- **Digital Twin Risk Simulation**: Runs Monte Carlo simulations (1,000 trials) to stress-test supply chains against lead-time shocks.
- **Smart Sourcing Optimizer**: Recommends multi-vendor allocation splits based on cost, delivery speed, and quality ratings.

## ⚡ 4. Quick 5-Minute Walkthrough Scenario

If you want to test the full lifecycle right now:

1. Go to **MRP Planner** $\rightarrow$ Click **Run MRP Explosion** $\rightarrow$ Click **Generate Purchase Requisitions**.
2. Go to **Dashboard** $\rightarrow$ Under **Pending Approvals**, click **Approve** on `PR-2026-0001`.
3. Go to **Procurement** $\rightarrow$ Find `PR-2026-0001` $\rightarrow$ Click **Raise PO**.
4. Click **101 Receipt** next to the newly created PO.
5. In **Goods Movements**, click **Post Goods Movement**.
6. **Watch the magic**: The PO turns `RECEIVED`, your warehouse inventory increases, and shortage warnings clear automatically!
